# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. We expect everyone to interact respectfully and help keep collaboration constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous, patient, and open to good-faith feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not on a person's identity or character.
- Explain disagreements clearly and accept that maintainers may choose a different solution.
- Respect privacy and share only information that you have permission to disclose.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and its educational purpose.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without consent is prohibited. Unwelcome conduct through project issues, pull requests, commits, or other project spaces is covered by this policy.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit, decline, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They will aim to explain moderation decisions clearly and handle reports discreetly.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, Brajesh Kumar (@brajesh31), through a private contact channel listed on the GitHub profile. Do not put private details in a public issue. Include links or context that help the maintainer understand the concern, and avoid forwarding sensitive material beyond what is needed.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository's issue and pull request discussions and other spaces where a person is representing this project. It applies to maintainers, contributors, and participants.

## Attribution

This is a project-specific code of conduct written for this repository.
